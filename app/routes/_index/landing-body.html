Frequently Asked Questions

Merchant Questions & Answers

How does the AI Assistant know my store's information? expand_more

The AI Assistant reads the live scan results of your store, including overall Health Score, issue categories, missing fields, and collection structure. It is strictly constrained to store health, catalog fixes, SEO, and inventory best practices. It never hallucinates data that does not exist.

Will CorePilot AI alter my theme code or disrupt my checkout? expand_more

No. CorePilot AI communicates safely through official Shopify GraphQL APIs to update product meta fields, SEO attributes, and media alt-texts. We do not edit your theme liquid or checkout files, and every change can be reversed with a single click.

Can I test paid plans on development stores? expand_more

Yes! Shopify allows development stores to test paid plans at $0. Live stores are charged normal monthly amounts directly through the standard Shopify billing system in Settings → Billing.

What happens if I downgrade or uninstall? expand_more

All fixes previously saved to your products, collections, and images remain completely intact inside your Shopify store. No reinstall or developer cleanup is required.
